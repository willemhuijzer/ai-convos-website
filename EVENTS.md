## Adding an edition

Create one numbered Markdown file under `events/` and link it above. Keep information that applies only to that edition in its file; keep shared project language in `CONTEXT.md` and durable agent guidance in `AGENTS.md`.

Use these evidence labels consistently:

- **Confirmed** — stated in direct organizer notes or a dated invitation and not contradicted by a stronger source.
- **Provisional** — announced or displayed, but still subject to change or contradicted elsewhere.
- **Unknown** — not established by the available material.
- **Conflicting** — multiple available sources make incompatible claims.
