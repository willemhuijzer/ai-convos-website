# AI Convos

AI Convos is a small Amsterdam event series where people with substantial AI experience exchange practical ideas and questions. This glossary keeps historical and future event content in one shared language.

## Language

**AI Convos**:
The current umbrella name for the series and its community, including editions previously promoted under other names.
_Avoid_: treating AI Sessions or AI Connect as unrelated series

**Edition**:
One numbered gathering in the AI Convos series. An edition has its own date, venue, host, format, speakers, and source material.
_Avoid_: event, meetup, version when identifying a numbered gathering

**Talk**:
A short contribution that gives the room enough context to discuss one concrete idea, experiment, failure, or lesson.
_Avoid_: keynote, pitch, lecture

**Speaker**:
The person sharing a talk and taking part in the discussion that follows.
_Avoid_: presenter, expert

**Host**:
The organization that welcomes the edition into its space. The host is not necessarily an organizer or venue owner.
_Avoid_: venue

**Venue**:
The physical place and address where an edition is held.
_Avoid_: host

**Format**:
The structure used by a particular edition, including talks, questions, questionnaires, and informal conversation. It can evolve between editions.
_Avoid_: assuming one edition's structure is a permanent template

**Questionnaire**:
A pre-event audience survey whose aggregated answers are used to show who is in the room and how that group uses and thinks about AI.
_Avoid_: registration form, feedback survey
